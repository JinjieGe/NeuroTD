NeuroTD 2.4.2 local reanalysis, frozen before new simulation results
Date: 2026-11-08. Retrospective supplementary analysis, NOT preregistration.

Scope: original sub-01, three sessions, 150 trials, original four label-defined groups.
Original estimator, E, signal models, previous seeds and project files remain untouched.
Input: verified trial_region_summary.csv. Original estimand: pooled nonzero absolute-delay
ratio differences, six comparisons. All group values from a trial stay together.
Anatomical labels remain unverified; do not make regional population claims.

Methods evaluated without selecting by real-data p:
1. Session-equal t test, three session-specific ratio differences, df=2, Bonferroni six.
   Different estimand; requires independent approximately Gaussian session estimates
   with a common contrast expectation. Not three independent participants.
2. Delete-one-session jackknife SE around ORIGINAL pooled difference, t df=2.
   A sensitivity analysis, not automatically valid with three unequal clusters.
3. Original pooled ratio + Bartlett b=1 HAC SE, with normal reference (diagnostic baseline).
4. Same HAC statistic with Student-t df=2 as a conservative candidate; df=2 is a
   chosen reference, NOT an estimated effective df or an established theorem for this statistic.
5. Same pooled statistic calibrated against the maximum absolute standardized estimation
   error across all six contrasts in each legacy model. Empirical p is the maximum tail
   probability over the ten fixed scenarios. A prespecified factor 1.15 on reference
   scores adds conservatism. This is a finite-model sensitivity envelope, NOT a
   distribution-free real-data p or a universal correction. Calibration nuisance parameters
   are fitted from the existing real table; independent simulated draws do not make them
   independently validated nuisance parameters.

Calibration: reuse unchanged legacy generators A,B1,B2,C1,C2,D1-D5; 20,000 draws each,
global null, new master seed 202611080242, stage=1. Reference scores are centered on
known generating contrasts, never on observed real effects. Translation invariance checked.
Independent validation: 20,000 draws each scenario x G/P1/P2 (600,000 total), stage=2.
All methods evaluated on common draws. Full family error and coverage reported, not just
the most favorable scenario. Nominal family levels .05,.01,.001. CP 95% upper bound
<=1.25*alpha is the legacy operational gate, NOT proof of exact alpha control.
95% simultaneous interval coverage reported with CP bounds. No changing gate after results.
Session-equal method is evaluated against its OWN population target, not pooled-ratio truth.
C2 session ratio finite-sample bias and session heterogeneity must be disclosed.
Analytic/session methods retain assumptions even if simulation gate passes.

Out-of-library challenges (stage=3; 20,000 draws each; no recalibration):
E1: independent sessions, AR phi=.97 with real SD/correlation and fixed N.
E2: C1 plus a shared across-session random group offset (SD=.2 ms).
These expose model extrapolation and dependence between sessions, not opportunities to
quietly enlarge the favorable set or tune the procedure.

Pilot: stage=99, 1,000 records per legacy scenario, no formal inference from pilot.
One worker; BLAS one thread; preserve competing jobs. Checkpoints at scenario granularity.
Audit hashes input and legacy inference/generator and original estimator before/after.
Primary delivery: evidence-based resolution, including null/limited findings as needed,
Chinese analysis, bilingual response and manuscript replacement text in standalone files.
No automatic edit to manuscript or connected response document.
If all methods remain conditional/unsupported, explicitly withdraw original Welch inference;
do not report an invented universal effective sample size or guarantee significance.

References: Ibragimov & Muller (2010), doi:10.1198/jbes.2009.08046,
https://www.princeton.edu/~umueller/tstat.pdf (session t assumptions only).
